## 🎯 **Key Concepts Introduced**

### **Human Entropy**
The revolutionary idea that humans can cryptographically own and control the randomness that drives their digital systems, from AI to cryptography to system operations.

### **Sovereign Randomness**
A new computing paradigm where randomness is not a machine-controlled resource but a human-owned, transferable, and auditable digital asset.

### **Ecosystem Sovereignty**
The extension of human ownership across entire computing ecosystems, ensuring human dignity and control are preserved at scale.
