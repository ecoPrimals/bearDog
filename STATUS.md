### January 3, 2026 (Afternoon) - Progressive Trust Model 🔒

**Multi-Level Capability-Based Security**:
- ✅ **4 Trust Levels**: None (0) → Limited (1) → Elevated (2) → Highest (3)
- ✅ **Capability Restrictions**: Fine-grained access control per level
- ✅ **Trust Elevation API**: POST /api/v1/trust/elevate with evidence validation
- ✅ **Operation-Specific Evaluation**: requested_operation field in requests
- ✅ **Wildcard Patterns**: "data/*" matches "data/read", "data/write"
- ✅ **Elevation Paths**: Guidance on how to increase trust
- ✅ **Human Oversight**: Required for federation (Level 2+)
- ✅ **Security**: Compromised USB → Limited blast radius (coordination only)
- ✅ **Time**: 3 hours (design + implementation + testing)

**The "BirdSong Analogy"**:
- Level 1: Can hear the song, NOT enter the nest
- Level 2: Can enter the nest (human approved)
- Level 3: Trusted family member (human entropy)
