## 1. What Lysogeny Does Here

**Lysogeny** is the wateringHole strategy for *area denial through open prior art*:
publish the mathematics and architecture in the open so proprietary claims
cannot fence the commons. BearDog’s role is **sovereign genetic cryptography** —
cryptographic lineage, tunnel encryption, and hardware-backed key material tied
to that model.

This repository implements those ideas in public. That publication is
intentional prior art: it documents how the ecosystem derives and protects keys
so third parties cannot later assert monopoly rights over the same design
space.

## 2. BearDog Software: AGPL-3.0-or-later

All BearDog **software** in this repository is licensed under **GNU Affero
General Public License v3.0 or later** (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). Under the AGPL, **use of
the software to provide a network service counts as distribution**: modified
versions used to run services must be made available to users as **corresponding
source**. That closes the “SaaS loophole” and keeps server-side deployments
accountable the same way binary distribution is.

Together with publication of the cryptographic design, AGPL ensures the
**sovereign compute stack** — the auditable crypto path primals rely on — cannot
be captured as a proprietary silo while still benefiting from community
improvements.
