## 🚨 **CRITICAL ARCHITECTURAL FIX**

### **Problem Identified**
- **Hardcoded primal names** (Songbird, NestGate, ToadStool) violated ecosystem sovereignty
- **Static dependencies** prevented organic ecosystem evolution
- **Large monolithic RPC file** (1,176 lines) hindered maintainability

### **✅ Resolution Implemented**
- **Removed ALL hardcoded primal references** from codebase
- **Implemented capability-based discovery** system
- **Split RPC into 5 modular files**: 
  - `models.rs` - Dynamic discovery data structures
  - `primal_registration.rs` - Self-registration & capability discovery
  - `ecosystem_collaboration.rs` - Service provision via capabilities
  - `cross_primal_operations.rs` - Resource requests through capability matching
  - `placeholder_handlers.rs` - Additional ecosystem operations
  - `mod.rs` - Clean routing and organization

### **Impact**
- ✅ **True ecosystem sovereignty** - no primal has hardcoded knowledge of others
- ✅ **Dynamic evolution support** - new primals can join/split/evolve organically
- ✅ **Maintainable codebase** - clear separation of concerns
